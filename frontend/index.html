Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a PDF exam into individual questions?

Upload your PDF exam to Exam Crop, and our AI automatically detects question boundaries and splits each question into a separate PDF file. The process takes just seconds.

What exam formats does Exam Crop support?

Exam Crop works with IGCSE, IB, SAT, Thanaweya Amma, Moasser exams, and custom worksheets. We support PDF, JPG, PNG, and HEIC file formats up to 20 pages and 20MB.

Is Exam Crop free for teachers?

Exam Crop is currently free during our early access phase. We're refining the platform and will introduce tiered plans with different limits in the future.

How accurate is the automatic question detection?

Our AI model is trained on thousands of real exam papers from different exam boards, achieving 85–90% accuracy across IGCSE, IB, SAT, and other standardized test formats.

Can I use Exam Crop for worksheets and homework assignments?

Yes! Exam Crop works with any structured worksheet or assignment where questions are clearly separated. It's perfect for creating individual practice problems for students.

What happens to my uploaded files?

Your files are processed securely and stored to help improve our AI model's accuracy across diverse exam formats. This helps us better serve teachers worldwide.
