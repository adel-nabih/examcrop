What is ExamCrop?

ExamCrop is a free AI tool that automatically detects and extracts individual questions from IGCSE exam papers and worksheets. Instead of manually cropping question 10a, 10b, 10c from a functions paper, you upload the file and ExamCrop does it in seconds.

It works across all IGCSE subjects — Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, and more. Whether you're building a question bank, preparing targeted practice sets, or putting together revision material, ExamCrop removes the tedious part so you can focus on teaching.

The model is custom-trained on IGCSE paper layouts, so it understands the structure — question numbers, sub-parts, marks, diagrams — and splits accordingly. Not a general-purpose PDF tool. Built specifically for this.
