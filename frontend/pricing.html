Questions

Is ExamCrop really free right now?
Yes — completely free during our beta phase with no limits. We're still refining the model and adding features. Paid plans are coming, but beta users will get an early-bird discount when they launch.
What happens to my free account when paid plans launch?
You'll automatically stay on the free tier. Nothing changes unless you choose to upgrade. If you signed up during beta, you'll get an exclusive discount on paid plans.
When are Basic and Pro launching?
We're targeting month two of the beta. Sign up for the notify list above and you'll be emailed the moment it goes live — with a discount for being early.
Will there be annual pricing?
Yes. Annual plans will be available at a discount — roughly equivalent to getting two months free. Details at launch.
Is there a plan for schools or departments?
A school tier with team seats is on the roadmap. If you're interested in a department-level plan, reach out at support@examcrop.com and we'll sort something out.
