What is ExamCrop?

ExamCrop is a free AI tool that automatically detects and extracts individual questions from SAT practice papers and worksheets. Instead of manually cropping each question for your students, you upload the file and ExamCrop handles it in seconds.

It works across all SAT sections — Math, Reading, Writing and Language. Whether you're building targeted practice sets by question type, isolating specific skill areas, or preparing focused revision material for a student, ExamCrop removes the prep time so you can focus on tutoring.

The model is trained on SAT paper layouts and understands the structure — question numbers, answer choices, passage references — and splits accordingly.
